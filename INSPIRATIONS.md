# Inspirations

Vorasion was inspired by quite a few things around the vore community, this file is meant to list them here.

- VoreBot | A really intriguing bot I found when exploring the Discord server of a vore forum. Albeit some of the ideas were cheesy and there's some bugs, it was amazing to draw inspiration from for the `/stomach handle` command, and possibly some future commands!
- Struggle Simulator | This was a really cool vore game to see and get inspired from for the mechanics of the dice minigame.
- Daily Gluttony 1 & 2 | This was also a fun vore game, really nice to bring anthro species ideas into my mind.
- **The greater vore community, they're genuinely really welcoming. In more ways than one.** ;)

Thanks to all of these, Vorasion exists as my magnum opus. My best attempt at making a Discord bot,
despite every bit of controversy surrounding the platform. I appreciate all of these. :)
